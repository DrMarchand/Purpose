# Authorship, Copyright, Publication, and Marks

Effective: 2026-08-16  
Status: Current repository rights baseline

## Default rule

- **Author and default copyright owner:** Joseph Kyle Marchand.
- **Design Orchard LLC:** legal and operating company. It may maintain, contract for, distribute, or publish a work only where a work-specific record documents that role.
- Repository location, branch or DBA association, operating responsibility, invoice language, publisher credit, and Git history do not by themselves transfer copyright.
- A different copyright owner requires a work-specific written instrument or other legally sufficient ownership record linked from the artifact or rights registry.
- Third-party code, documentation, fonts, media, and other assets retain their original notices, licenses, and ownership.

## Publication record

A work attributed to Design Orchard LLC as publisher must identify the work, author, version, publication date, and the record supporting the publisher role. Publisher status does not by itself make Design Orchard LLC the copyright owner.

## Marks

Trademark status is separate from copyright. `TM` or `SM` indicates a claim, not a registration. The registered symbol may be used only when a current registration record supports the exact mark and applicable goods or services. Candidate, crowded, historical, and internal names must not be represented as registered or cleared without mark-specific evidence.
